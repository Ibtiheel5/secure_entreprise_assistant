# Incident Report 2026-03

## Summary

On 14 March 2026 a phishing email led to the compromise of one employee mailbox at Nordia Systems.

## Root cause

The root cause was a missing multi-factor authentication policy on the legacy mail gateway.

## Impact

No customer data was exfiltrated. Approximately 40 internal emails were read by the attacker.

## Remediation

MFA was enforced on all mail accounts and the legacy gateway was decommissioned on 21 March.

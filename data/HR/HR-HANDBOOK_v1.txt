# Employee Handbook

## Purpose

This employee handbook describes working conditions at Nordia Systems. It applies to every employee in all departments.

## Working hours

Standard working hours at Nordia Systems are 9:00 to 17:00. Employees take a one hour lunch break.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

## Dress code

Business casual clothing is expected in client-facing meetings.
